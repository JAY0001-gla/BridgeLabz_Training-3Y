Top 3 Travel Destinations

Agra

Agra is a popular historical city in India and is famous for the Taj Mahal.

A beautiful place for people who enjoy history and architecture.

Jaipur

Jaipur is known for its forts, palaces, colourful markets and traditional Rajasthani culture.

The old city has a unique mix of history, food and local culture.

Goa

Goa is a popular destination for beaches, relaxed evenings and coastal scenery.

Goa is a good choice when you want a relaxed beach holiday.

Travel Tips

  • Carry important documents safely.
  • Check the weather before travelling.
  • Keep some local currency with you.
  • Respect local places and traditions.
